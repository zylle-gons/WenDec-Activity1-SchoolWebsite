DEPED CURRICULUMS

The Department of Education's (DepEd) current basic education framework is the MATATAG Curriculum, a streamlined and decongested version of the K to 10 program designed to focus heavily on foundational skills

Junior High School(JHS)

Junior High School (JHS) encompassing Grades 7, 8, 9, and 10 represents Key Stage 3. In this stage, the framework transitions from primary foundational learning toward an increased focus on specialized subjects, critical thinking, higher-order thinking, and career or work readiness

Senior High School(SHS)

The Department of Education (DepEd) has officially restructured the Grades 11 and 12 program through the Strengthened Senior High School (SHS) Curriculum. This framework was created to complement the K–10 MATATAG Curriculum by resolving structural congestion, expanding elective opportunities, and aligning directly with industry demands

Special Curricular Programs

The Department of Education offers Special Curricular Programs (SCPs) designed to help students maximize their distinct talents, strengths, and career interests alongside the standard basic education framework. Following the rollout of the MATATAG Curriculum, DepEd officially released Revised Curriculum Guides for the Special Curricular Programs to seamlessly align these unique specializations with the streamlined K-to-12 standards.
